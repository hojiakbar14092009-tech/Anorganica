# Exam blueprint — Milliy sertifikat, Kimyo

- Primary source: https://www.uzbmb.uz/upload/file/pdf/mtt/spetsifikatsiya/Kimyo-MS2025.pdf (10 pages). Checked 2026-11-15.
- Confirmed from the primary source on that date: total time 180 min = 100 min (tasks 1–40) + 80 min (tasks 41–43, written); task types Y1, Y2, O1, O2;
  task 41 general chemistry, 42 organic chemistry, 43 inorganic chemistry or chemical analysis; O2 tasks are 25 points each (75 total);
  Y1/Y2/O1 are scored with the Rasch model.
- Taken from the user-supplied verified blueprint (not re-extracted per type on 2026-11-15): 43 tasks; Y1 × 32 (general 13, inorganic 6, organic 10, analysis 3); Y2 × 3; O1 × 5; O2 × 3.
- Sample portal: https://uzbmb.uz/page/namuna_m (not used as a question source).

## Coverage policy
Grade 8–9 textbooks cover general + inorganic chemistry only partially and no organic chemistry. The app offers
“8–9-sinf mavzulari bo‘yicha sertifikat mashqlari”, never a 43-task simulation, never an official/Rasch score.
